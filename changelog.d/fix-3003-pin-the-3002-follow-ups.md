### Fixed — a receipt check counts the proof of a send typed but not submitted while it waits for ingest (#3003)

`claudlobby message receipt` answers `unavailable` (exit 6) while a transmission proof for the message is staged and not yet ingested, and not `missing` (exit 8) or `no_history` (exit 9). A send typed into the recipient's box and not submitted records an `unknown` transmission that carries the transport's wire proof (#3001), and the delivery join reads that proof once the receiver's own row lands. While that row was staged, the check did not count it, so a message whose receipt had not landed read as having none. It now counts a staged tmux `unknown` transmission that carries a wire proof, as it counts a staged `received` or `pane_submitted` one. The retry gate does not: an `unknown` transmission still never refuses a resend. Tests now pin three behaviours that none read before: the join reads the newest `unknown` transmission that carries a proof, a `carrier_queued` transmission counts as submitted, and a replay refuses every difference between the retained and the recorded transmission except a missing proof on an `unknown` one. Two comments that said the wire proof rides only a submission-class transmission now say it also rides a tmux `unknown` one.
