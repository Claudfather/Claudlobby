### Changed — tests pin three behaviours of the delivery join and the replay guard, and a failed record of a typed send queues nothing (#3003)

Tests now pin three behaviours that none read before: the join reads the newest `unknown` transmission that carries a proof, a `carrier_queued` transmission counts as submitted, and a replay refuses every difference between the retained and the recorded transmission except a missing proof on an `unknown` one. A fourth test pins that the record of a send typed into the box and not submitted, which carries the transport's wire proof (#3001), writes no spool or staged entry when it fails, and that a replay records the row and sends nothing again. Two comments that said the wire proof rides only a submission-class transmission now say it also rides a tmux `unknown` one.
