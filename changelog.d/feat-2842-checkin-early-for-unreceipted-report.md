### Fixed — the check-in beat comes before its minimum gap, once, for a report whose notice has no receipt (#2842)

A worker's report reaches its manager by one send, made right after the report is recorded. When that send is refused, because the manager's input box held text, a menu was open or its session was missing, nothing sends it again, and the manager check-in beat still waited out its minimum gap (45 minutes by default) before the manager next read its reports. Now, inside the gap, the beat comes early when the Plane holds a report addressed to the manager that it has not acknowledged, recorded after its last check-in, with no receipt for its notice. The check-in that beat records is newer than the report, so each such report brings the beat early once, and the next tick waits its gap. The two busy checks, the one send, its refusal on a held box or an open menu, and the keys it sends are unchanged. A receipt for the manager still queued for Plane ingest, a manager that has never recorded a receipt, or a read that cannot answer keeps the gap. The read is `plane-lookup.py --checkin-early`; the beat's log line names the report. Part of #2842.
