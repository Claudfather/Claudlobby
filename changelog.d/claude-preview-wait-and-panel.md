### Fixed — Setup page: a slow "Preview team" no longer sends you backwards or strands you

On a small computer, "Preview team" can take longer than the 15 seconds the computer allowed for it. The work still finished and was kept, but the page showed "Could not prepare the preview, so nothing was saved", dropped back to "Approve setup", and lost the tick on "Sign in to Claude", though the computer still had Claude connected. "Nothing was saved" was also untrue: the prepared team was on the computer. Reloading then left no way to continue, because the page knew a preview existed but not what was in it.

Preview now gets 90 seconds on the computer (the page waits 95, so it never gives up first); Launch keeps its 45. When a preview fails, the page keeps Connect, Approve setup and Sign in to Claude ticked and keeps your goal in the box, then asks the computer what became of the preview. If the computer finished it, the page shows the plan and "Launch team", exactly as if the answer had arrived; this also works after a reload, because the computer's reply to "Check setup approval" now carries the kept plan. If the computer began preparing but did not finish, the page says only the computer can restart that and shows the command. If it never got started, the page says so and "Preview team" works again. Permissions and approval rules are unchanged.
