### Fixed — Setup page: a slow "Preview team" no longer sends you backwards or strands you

On a small computer, "Preview team" can take longer than the 15 seconds the computer allowed for it. The work still finished and was kept, but the page showed "Could not prepare the preview, so nothing was saved", dropped back to "Approve setup", and lost the tick on "Sign in to Claude", though the computer still had Claude connected. "Nothing was saved" was also untrue: the prepared team was on the computer. Reloading then left no way to continue, because the page knew a preview existed but not what was in it.

The computer now says what became of a preview. Its reply to "Check setup approval" carries `preview_state` for this approval only: `none` (no preview), `preparing` (its update record is absent or still in its prepare phase with no stage error, Preview is still running on this computer, and less time has passed than its three stage processes may take: 3 × 1200 s + 300 s), `ready` (with the kept plan), `expired` (prepared, but its 10-minute window closed before Launch), or `failed` (a stage error, Preview no longer running, or that time passed). Before, that reply was refused while preparation ran, because the update record has no configuration fingerprint until preparation finalizes. Only this approval's own record still in its prepare phase is now admitted, and only for this reply. A record with a different target, scope or request, any other approval, and Preview, Launch and status checks are refused exactly as before.

When a preview fails or its answer is lost, the page keeps Approve setup and Sign in to Claude ticked and keeps your goal, then asks the computer once and follows its answer. While the computer is still preparing, the page checks again every 5 seconds for up to 10 minutes and says when it stops; "Check preview" looks again. A ready preview shows the plan and "Launch team", also after a reload. An expired preview says this page can no longer launch it and shows the command that launches it from the computer. A failed one says so and shows the command to retry it there, when the computer has one. If the computer has no preview, the page says whether the computer refused to start it or it never started, and "Preview team" can be sent again, without promising that this will work. A refused check reads differently from one that got no answer, and neither removes the ticks. If the connection changes while the page is checking, it says the checks stopped.

Preview gets 90 seconds on the computer's listener (the page waits 95); Launch keeps its 45. Layers in front of the listener can still end the request sooner: the website's in-browser connection allows 15 seconds, and the SSH route (`owner_api_stdio.py`) still ends every call at 45 seconds. The page then follows the computer's answer as above. Permissions, approval rules and Launch's own checks are unchanged.
