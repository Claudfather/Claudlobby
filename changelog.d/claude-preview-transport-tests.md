### Changed — the setup size ceiling is tested with a realistic first-team Preview and Context answer

`tests/plane_connected_workspace.test.mjs` and `tests/plane_connected_read.test.mjs` now carry a synthetic first-team plan of the measured real shape (about 63 KB: 76 changes, 70 units without their working data) as both the `setup-preview` answer and the `setup-context` answer that repeats it. It passes through the browser transport and the connected read client, and the setup panel reaches the ready state and offers Launch team. An answer of exactly 131,072 bytes passes and one byte more is refused: the transport retires, the read client rejects, and the panel says "Could not get an answer from your computer about the preview" and offers Check preview. Before, setup answers in these tests were a few hundred bytes. Tests only: no product code, limit or string changes.
