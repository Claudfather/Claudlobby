### Changed — with one team on one computer, the connected view folds the teams list and the computer's parts to one line each

A connected view now marks on `main` the computers it lists (`data-computers`, from `connected-workspace.js`), and `app.js` marks how many teams the view has, hidden ones included (`data-teams`). A view that lists no computers, before its account check or once it retires, clears both marks. When both are 1, `workspace.css` folds the teams list to one line (the team's choice with its status words, and the Bots in this team and Computers & status drawers) and the parts strip above the room to one wrapping line, so the conversation and the Message team composer take the rest of the height. All, Hide and the hidden-teams toggle, which change nothing with one team, are left out of the line, and on a phone so is the Teams heading. A drawer opened on the line drops below it. On a wide screen the line with an open drawer takes at most a quarter of the column (15% while the composer is open) and scrolls inside itself, so the Message team row stays in view at the website's smallest frame.

Behaviour change: Computers & status still opens itself when its computer asks for attention, and in the fold a drawer the page opened that way now closes again when the connected view says that attention cleared (`onConnectionAttentionCleared`), or as soon as the fold shows if the team was not known yet. It stays open once the person has pressed or focused anything in it, or closed it themselves, and a drawer the person opened is never closed for them. With two or more teams or computers, or in a view that is not a connected one, the layout and the drawer are unchanged. No words, colours or motion change, and the fold sets no fixed or viewport height. The design is described in `documentation/architecture/owner-connected-read.md` ("One team on one computer").
