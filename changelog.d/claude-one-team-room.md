### Changed — with one team on one computer, the connected view folds the teams list and the computer's parts to one line each

A connected view now marks on `main` the computers it lists (`data-computers`, from `connected-workspace.js`) and `app.js` marks the teams its list shows (`data-teams`). When both are 1, `workspace.css` folds the teams list to one line (the team's choice and the Bots in this team and Computers & status drawers) and the parts strip above the room to one wrapping line, so the conversation and the Message team composer take the rest of the height. All, Hide and the hidden-teams toggle, which change nothing with one team, are left out of the line; on a phone so are the Teams heading and the team's choice, whose name heads the room just below. A drawer opened on the line drops below it. Computers & status still opens itself when its computer asks for attention; in the fold it closes again once that computer is ready, unless keyboard focus is in it, and a drawer the person opened or reopened stays as they left it. With two or more teams or computers, or in a view that is not a connected one, the layout is unchanged. No words, colours or motion change, and the fold sets no fixed or viewport height. The design is described in `documentation/architecture/owner-connected-read.md` ("One team on one computer").
