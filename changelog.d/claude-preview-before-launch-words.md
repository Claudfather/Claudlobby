### Changed — after Preview team, the setup panel says plainly what Launch does and keeps the counts under Technical details

After a Preview, the setup panel's banner read "Preview ready. Review it below, then choose Launch team." and the line above Launch team gave counts of generated files and bots. A person found that the banner pointed at nothing plain and that the counts meant nothing. With the words the owner approved on 2026-11-24, the banner now reads "Your team is ready to launch.". Above Launch team, outside the drawer, the panel says "Launch starts your lead, engineer and reviewer on this computer. The lead plans the work, the engineer does it and the reviewer checks it. Merging, deploying, extra spending and destructive changes each need your permission first." Every first team a Preview prepares comes from one of the two package templates. Each is a lead, an engineer and a reviewer, and each works under the task-scoped-effects guardrail, which asks the person first for each of those four actions. "Bots already running here restart briefly." shows there as well, but only when the plan's `restart_bots` names a bot outside the team being launched. A first-team Preview names none today. The drawer is now called Technical details, as on the website's permission cards. It holds the count sentence, without its leading "Your team is ready.", along with the app version and the raw plan. Once the Preview has run out, the banner goes, so "ready" no longer shows beside "This preview expired". What each action sends, and when, is unchanged.
