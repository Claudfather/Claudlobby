### Changed — the team view gives the conversation the screen (#NNNN)

The "Message team" composer now starts folded to its one summary row, a native disclosure that keyboards and screen readers reach and announce as before; it opens from that row, from Message bot or from a task action, and coming back from Tasks leaves it as it was. On wide screens the conversation keeps at least 45% of its column (30% while someone is in the composer) and the composer scrolls inside itself after that; the room heading and filters take about one row less. The column now fills whatever the header leaves, so the composer's bottom is no longer cut off when something sits above the header. On phones the page is the only scroller: the teams list, team column and composer no longer scroll inside fixed-height boxes, each team's Hide sits beside it, and the folded composer row waits at the bottom of the screen. A wide-screen side-space rule that never applied is removed. No wording, colour or font changed; the setup-only view is unchanged.
