### Changed — the view served from a person's computer connects by itself once, right after setup hands over (gated on the owner's decision)

Draft, waiting on the owner's yes (decision 11-02): do not ship before it. When the website's in-page connection carries the view, the view now connects to the bound computer once by itself, instead of waiting for a press on Connect. It does this only when the computer is admitted, the account check is ready, the read says it is not connected, nothing was tried yet in that page's lifetime, and the page is visible (a hidden page waits until it is shown, still at most once). It never connects by itself after an expiry, a failure, an unknown outcome, a denial or Disconnect, so a tab left open cannot hold the computer's session forever (computers cap sessions at 32). After any of those, Connect stays a press, exactly as before. A view opened directly, outside the website's carrier, is unchanged. The first connect runs the same follow-up reads as a pressed Connect (the setup approval read and a kept launch's receipt read) without moving keyboard focus. Three hints lose the word "again" because the person has not yet pressed Connect: "Could not connect to this computer ... choose Connect again", "This computer did not accept the connection ... choose Connect again" and "... its host outcome is unknown; choose Connect again explicitly". Core owns this contract; the website follows by pin.
