### Fixed — log rotation leaves a log at or under its cap alone, keeps a cut log's mode, and no longer reaches a bot's `data/` files by name (#3165)

The daily `log-rotation` job rewrote every log it was given, under the cap too: `log-rotate.sh` tailed each one into a temp file and renamed that over it, so a 100-line log came back every day as a new inode with new times, and each rewritten log took the temp file's mode, 0600, so a log made group-readable turned owner-only. It now counts a log's lines first and leaves a log of 500 lines or fewer alone, with the same inode, mode, owner and times. A longer one is still cut to its last 500 lines, and the temp takes the log's mode before the rename, read by the new `stat_mode` helper in `lib-common.sh` with GNU or BSD `stat`.

The job also searched each bot's `data/` to depth 3 for `cron.log`, `git-pull.log`, `briefing*.log` and `home-assistant.log`, and the weekly `data-sweep` deleted those names anywhere under `data/` once they were 30 days unwritten. No framework job writes a log under a bot's `data/`, so both matched the bot's own files by name: a record kept as `data/archive/q1/briefing-notes.log` was cut to 500 lines. Rotation now reaches each bot's root and `logs/` and the host's `state/logs/`, never `data/`, and the sweep purges no log by name; it still ages out `*.bak` copies and the RC-relay markers. The two change together, because the rotation fix alone would let the next sweep delete every such file the daily rewrite had kept fresh.
