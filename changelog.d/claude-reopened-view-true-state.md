### Fixed — a setup page opened again after a Launch lands on the launch's true state after Connect (#3097)

A page reopened after "Team launched" showed "Nothing runs on this computer until you choose Launch team." beside a running team, and after Connect it showed Approve setup and Launch your team as not done until the person pressed Check launch status. When the computer says (`launch_reserved`) that the kept request's Launch is reserved, the browser now keeps that word beside the request. A page opened again hides that line from its first paint, and after a panel Connect it does the Check launch status read once by itself, ending on Team launched or the launch's unfinished state. That read changes nothing on the computer and is not retried; if it fails or is refused, the view stays as it was and shows nothing. It never runs beside a Preview that can be launched or while the hand-over is already reading. A request kept from a Preview alone gets no such read. Nothing is launched, renewed or retried, and no sentence was added or changed.
