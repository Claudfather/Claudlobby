### Changed — after a marked Launch on Linux, the computer starts the setup finish itself

After a Launch whose team card carried the messages mark, the setup guide starts the terminal's existing setup waiter once, detached, where one shared check holds (a Linux service manager, not the managed Mac runtime, a launcher the waiter accepts). When the Launch is healthy and done, the waiter runs the same finish with no terminal: messages on for that team under the card's name and the card's goal sent to the lead once, and nothing else. This is the one exception to "owner changes require an interactive operator terminal"; an approval without the mark is still refused, and every other owner command still needs a terminal. The Launch result keeps exactly its five keys. Who will finish is recorded and reported by the status run as `setup.finish` (`starter` `computer`, or `page` with `not_supported` or `waiter_unavailable`), with `running` from a lock probe while the record says in progress, so a finish that died is read as stale. A waiter that cannot start never turns the Launch into an error; the page finishes. The waiter's output goes to `state/setup-finish-<id>.log` (created once, mode 0600) and carries neither the name nor the goal. One attempt, no retry. On a Mac nothing is started and the page finishes, as before. Not reachable from the website until its core pin moves. (#3117)
