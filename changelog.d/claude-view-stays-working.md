### Fixed — a launched team's view stays on the team when its session lapses (41-07)

On a website setup page, after a healthy Launch, the frame shows the team: its rooms, conversation and message box. A page left idle gets no input, so its 900-second session is not renewed and runs out. The view then dropped its parts answer and went back to the setup form. There the finished Launch ticked every step, Connect included, so no button was left and the team could not be reached again from the frame. Now a computer that showed the team in this page keeps that view through a lapse of its session: it runs out, a renewal's answer is lost, a Connect or check from the card is under way or a Connect does not reach the computer, or a new session's parts read has not answered. The card then offers Connect or Check connection, and the frame posts no new form or height. Any other end of the session (a refusal, a sign-out, a session check that ends anywhere but ready) still brings back the setup form, as does a parts answer with no team. In that form, after a finished Launch, the Connect step now stays current while the computer is not connected. No sentence changed.

### Fixed — a new message goes to the team's lead by default (41-08)

The message box's recipient list comes from the computer's message context, which lists the team's bots by name and marks no lead, so the first bot (often the engineer) was chosen by default. The view now uses the lead that the team's task contexts name, as Talk to manager already does. If that lead arrives after the message context, it replaces the default, unless the person chose a bot or wrote something. Only a bot the person chose or wrote to is remembered for the team. With no lead known, the first bot is still the default. No sentence changed.
