### Changed — Room for a longer Claude sign-in

A sign-in conversation now accepts up to 512 calls instead of 256. Before a sign-in starts, the setup page also renews its session unless more than 645 seconds remain, instead of 345. This is the first step toward a ten-minute window to paste the Claude code: a status check every two seconds for ten minutes needs about 320 calls, and the session that starts a sign-in has to outlast it. The attempt itself still lasts five minutes.
