### Added — A refused Claude sign-in records which rule refused Claude's reply

When the computer could not read Claude's reply during a browser sign-in, the attempt ended as `unsupported_output`, `ambiguous_output` or `output_limit`, and nothing said why. The computer now also keeps the name of the rule that refused the reply, in a new `detail` column of its sign-in record (`setup_provider`). It is a short fixed phrase such as `csi final z 5` (a terminal sequence it does not handle), `token shape`, or `exit 0 without token` (Claude finished without its success text). Someone at the computer can read it, for example with `sqlite3 -readonly` on `state/plane/owner-access.db` under the data root. Claude's reply itself is not stored: every phrase comes from one fixed list, and the most it can hold of the reply is a control code, or one letter and 16 digits or punctuation marks of what followed an escape character. The setup page is sent nothing new, and the reasons, messages and Try again are unchanged.

Finish or recover setup before downgrading. This version adds the column when it approves setup or starts a sign-in, and older versions refuse that table until it is recovered on the computer.
