### Fixed — `bot stop` says when the session it stopped is still exiting, and the lifecycle doors read that as temporary (#2981)

A bot unit runs `KillMode=process`, so systemd reads it stopped while the session it started is still exiting, and `bot stop` waits up to 30 s for that session to leave the unit's cgroup. When processes are still there once the wait ends, `bot stop` now says that it de-enrolled the bot and how many processes are left, keeps its record, writes its `bot_stopped` row (with `remaining_processes`), and refuses retryable, with a hint to wait 30 s and run it again. Until that session has gone, the enrollment inventory that `bot start`, `bot stop`, `bot restart`, the `fleet` sweeps and `fleet reconcile` read first refuses a bot whose stop is recorded by saying that its session is still exiting and that this is temporary, where it said "loaded consumer lacks installed source bytes", and those doors answer retryable with the same wait. A new native read, `svc_activation_cgroup_members`, counts the processes. A quiet check that refuses with no process counted reads as before.
