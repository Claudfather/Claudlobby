### Fixed — one issue's unreadable cross-references no longer fail the whole backlog read (#3101)

With `mergedReferences` asked for, `issue-intake.py list` reads each cross-reference's repository owner before its other fields and drops another owner's reference there, since only the owner's references are listed. An issue whose timeline runs past 10 pages, or with one of the owner's references in a shape the read does not take, now gets `null` in place of the list, with its number on stderr, and the repo's other issues are listed as usual: one issue's timeline no longer costs a check-in that repo's whole backlog for the beat. Step 4 of the `checkin` skill says what `null` means: whether work merged under that issue is unknown, not none. A failed read, or a reply not in the shape of its query, still refuses at exit 3.
