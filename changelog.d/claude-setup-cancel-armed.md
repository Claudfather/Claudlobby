### Fixed — a second click on Sign in to Claude can no longer cancel the sign-in it started

In the first-team setup panel, Sign in to Claude used to disappear as soon as it was clicked. Cancel sign-in, the only button while sign-in starts, then took its place, right under the pointer. A double-click or an impatient second click cancelled the sign-in it had just started. Sign in to Claude now stays in place, held and outlined, while the page checks the start automatically ("Starting Claude sign-in…"). Cancel sign-in sits in the row below it. Once the sign-in link is ready, Open Claude sign-in takes that place as before. After a check that could not reach the computer, the held button goes away and Check sign-in progress leads as before. What each action sends, and when, is unchanged.
