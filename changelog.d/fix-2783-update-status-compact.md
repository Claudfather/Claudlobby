### Changed — `host update status` prints a compact record led by `phase` and `target`, and `--full` prints the whole one (#2783)

`claudlobby host update status` printed the whole update record. On one host with 136 units that was 1,567,145 bytes, with `update.phase` at character 1,291,280, so a reader that sees only the start of a long output saw neither `phase` nor `target`. The status now leaves out the five collections that grow with the units and files an update touches (`consumers`, `scope`, `quiet`, `candidate_config` and `publication`) and counts them under `omitted`: each one's bytes and units or entries, and how many consumers are in each state of `park`, `stop`, `restore` and `checkpoint_state`. Every other field stays at its path, led by `phase` and `target`. Over the same record the status is 3,687 bytes. `record_written_at` is when the updater last saved the record, not when it reached its phase. `host update status --full` and `host update recovery --full` print the whole record exactly as before, and `--full` with `apply` or `continue` is refused. With no update recorded, the output is unchanged.
