### Added — the team card carries the name you message as, and after a healthy Launch messages turn on and the setup goal reaches the lead once (#3111)

In the guided setup, the team card (`allow_setup`) now carries a messages mark, its effects say so, and its answer carries the name the team will see (`actor_alias`, `human:<name>`). The computer checks the name with `sender_alias_refusal`: 2 to 40 ASCII lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens, starting with a letter and not ending with a hyphen, and not a reserved word such as `owner` or `lead` (`default-user` is an ordinary name). A refused name stops the guide with `sender_name_refused` and approves nothing. The approval stores the name. After a healthy Launch, the setup page's finish for such an approval takes that name, or the first free `<name>-2` to `<name>-9` if a person or bot already uses it, registers it, turns ordinary messages on for that team under it, and sends the Preview's goal to the lead once, recorded with the provenance `{"carrier": "setup_finish"}`. It re-checks the approval, the binding, the grant and the goal against the launched configuration before acting, counts the goal sent only from a matching receipt, and running it again grants and sends nothing more. `host owner setup-status` and the guide's status report the result. The setup view shows the exact goal above Launch team with the words that Launch sends it, and says after Launch that you can message the lead. The terminal card, unmarked approvals and their stored snapshots are unchanged. Inert until the website's core pin moves past this change, and that pin must move together with the website's name-card change: today's page answers the marked team card, approving or declining, without a name, and the guide refuses that answer with `invalid_request`. A finish typed at the terminal is recorded `by: terminal`; the computer starting the finish by itself is a later change.
