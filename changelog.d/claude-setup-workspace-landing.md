### Fixed — the website's setup frame opens on its computer's setup panel, with Connect as the first step

When the website opened the workspace for a computer through the browser carrier, the frame showed the whole workspace for every saved computer. The setup panel stayed hidden until that computer was connected, and the main area filled with "unavailable" summaries. The frame is now a setup-only view: it shows one panel, "Finish setting up <computer>", for the computer the website chose. Its first step, "Connect this computer", has one Connect button. Nothing connects, signs in or retries automatically. After Connect, the usual steps follow: approve setup, sign in to Claude, describe the goal, launch your team. A launch that finishes cleanly ends on a done step that says the team is running. The header, sidebar, rooms, tasks, Recorder, the Message team composer, other saved computers and their summaries are all hidden in this view. The panel fills the frame width and has no inner scroll. The frame reports its content height to the website, as `{type:"claudfather.workspace.size",version:1,height}`, so the website can size it. Failures and waits are plain sentences. The account check against every saved computer, each computer's connection, and every launch, receipt and sign-in safety check are unchanged. Native workspaces are unchanged.
