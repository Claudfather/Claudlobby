### Fixed — a silent keepalive no longer pages `activity_stuck` for every bot; it pages `keepalive_silent` once (#2862)

`activity_stuck` read the absence of keepalive's idle stamp as a running turn, so while a fleet's keepalive was silent fleet-pulse paged it for every bot that had made no tool call within the threshold. keepalive now stamps `data/.observed` on every tick that settles a verdict, and `data/.observed-since` on its first such tick and on the first after a silence longer than `OBSERVABILITY_KEEPALIVE_SILENT_THRESHOLD`. fleet-pulse judges `activity_stuck` only while `.observed` is fresh, and times a stall from the latest of the last tool call, the last tick with no turn running, the start of keepalive's current watch and the session's start: a late tick never restarts a hung turn's clock, and a new session or a long silence does.

fleet-pulse raises the new critical `keepalive_silent` FLEET ALERT once an episode, naming every live, supervised bot it has seen unwatched for `OBSERVABILITY_KEEPALIVE_SILENT_THRESHOLD` (default 600 s, set per bot in `bots.<name>.env`; `0` turns it off, and the sweep says so on stderr). It goes out again at once when another bot joins the episode, and on the re-notify window while the episode lasts. A stopped bot, one with no unit file and one with no session are never named: their own alerts report them.
