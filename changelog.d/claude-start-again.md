### Added — Start again: the installer can remove a never-started preparation so a newer revision can be prepared

`host-install-v1.sh` has two more actions, `start-again-status` and `start-again`, on Linux and Mac, taking the same request line as `prepare`. For a computer that was prepared (and may have had the guide's first steps approved) but whose team was never started, `start-again` moves its install, data, staging, host-policy, provider-bin and provider-profile folders into `family/start-again` and deletes them; `start-again-status` answers whether it would, writing nothing. They refuse by default, removing nothing: a sign that a team ran (a committed configuration, an update record, the updater's job, a unit, `plane.db`), a held lock, a receipt or checkout that is not this installer's, any name, kind, owner, mode or device the installer does not write, or a non-empty first-team folder. A crash after any step is finished by a second press. Everything outside the family folder, the Mac runtime, the first-team folder, units and the system tools are left alone. The installer grows by about 20 KB. Nothing calls the actions until the website admits them, so this is inert until then, and whether the owner wants Start again at all is still undecided.
