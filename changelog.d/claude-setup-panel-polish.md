### Changed — the setup panel's steps are checked off instead of numbered, its quiet actions are buttons, and its waits turn a ring

In the first-team setup panel, each step's number is replaced by a mark: an empty ring for a step to come, a sage dot for the current step and a ✓ once it is done. The website shows this panel inside its own numbered step, so the panel no longer adds a second numbered list there. Check again, Check sign-in progress, Cancel sign-in and Check launch status are small outlined buttons instead of underlined text, and each is still filled while it is its step's one lead action. A line that waits on the computer (Connecting…, Checking setup approval…, Starting Claude sign-in…, Code sent, finishing sign-in…, Checking automatically while your team connects… and the other operation lines) turns a small ring beside its text; with reduced motion the ring stands still. The passive "This page checks for progress automatically." note does not spin. The visible lines say "✓ Setup approved", "Your team works in …" and "Your team is ready." instead of naming the team's internal name, which stays under Details. What each action sends, and when, is unchanged.
