### Changed — the setup panel's steps are checked off instead of numbered, its quiet actions are buttons, and its waits turn a ring

In the first-team setup panel, each step's number is replaced by a mark that `workspace.css` draws from the step's state: an empty ring for a step to come, a sage dot for the current step and a ✓ once it is done. The website shows this panel inside its own numbered step, so the panel no longer adds a second numbered list there. Check sign-in progress, Cancel sign-in and Check launch status are outlined buttons like Copy link instead of underlined text, and Check again sits as a smaller outlined button on the "✓ Setup approved" line; each is still filled while it is its step's one lead action. A line that waits on the computer turns a small ring beside its text: every operation's progress line (Connecting…, Checking setup approval…, Sending your code… and the rest), "Checking automatically while your team connects…", the sign-in lines "Starting Claude sign-in…" and "Code sent, finishing sign-in…" while the computer finishes on its own, and the setup view's "Checking your workspace…" and "Opening the connection to your computer…". The passive "This page checks for progress automatically." note does not turn. The global reduced-motion rule now also reaches `::before` and `::after`, so the ring stands still there. The visible lines say "✓ Setup approved", "Your team works in …" and "Your team is ready." instead of naming the team's internal name, which stays under Details. What each action sends, and when, is unchanged.
