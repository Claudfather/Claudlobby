### Fixed — a late signal in one test can no longer end a whole CI shard

`tests/test_owner_stdio.py::test_typed_real_child_drain_records_repeated_signals_before_success` sends a signal to the pytest process twice while a child drains. The child drained for a fixed 150 milliseconds. On a slow runner the sender could be scheduled later than that, so its signal arrived after the handlers had been restored and ended pytest itself: exit 143 or 129, or a `KeyboardInterrupt` after every test had passed. Five macOS shards ended this way in the 60 pull-request runs from 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-21. The child now drains until the sender says both signals are out, so both arrive during the owned cleanup whatever the timing. A signal that still arrives afterwards is recorded and fails this one test. Tests only; no product code changes.
