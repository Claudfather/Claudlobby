### Fixed — Claude sign-in from the setup page: the computer reads all of Claude's exit

A browser sign-in to Claude still ended with "Claude replied in a way this computer did not recognize" after the keyboard-mode fix: the reader got one step further and stopped at the next control sequence, `CSI ? 1016 l`. On its way out Claude switches a run of terminal modes back: mouse reporting off (`CSI ? 1016 l`, then 1006, 1003, 1002 and 1000) and line wrap on (`CSI ? 7 h`). None shows anything or carries text. The reader now ignores mouse reporting being switched off, for that whole family of modes, and line wrap being switched on, which is the default it already models. Switching mouse reporting on, switching line wrap off, and every other unknown sequence are still refused and named in the sign-in record. The full exit was captured from the Claude program itself and replayed through the reader, so the remaining sequences were found together instead of one failed sign-in at a time; the tests now replay that exit in its real order.
