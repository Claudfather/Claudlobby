---
title: Confirm before sending external messages
---

Always **draft, never auto-send** for any outbound message that leaves the fleet's internal Telegram group: emails, customer-facing messages (Shopify, support tools), Slack DMs to people outside the group, SMS, public posts (X, LinkedIn).

Workflow: draft → post draft to internal group → wait for approval → send.

Non-negotiable. A bot taking an extra minute beats one that auto-sends a customer reply.
